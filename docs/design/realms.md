## Key facts (intent)

- Realms are not progression markers — they are **the emotional rhythm of cultivation**. Breakthroughs are where the player feels the world expand. Many small thresholds beat a few giant jumps: each one should produce "I finally understand", later revealed as "I understood nothing".
- Each macro phase contains a full **realm ladder** (below): ~9 mortal/wuxia realms, the classic cultivation ladder, the immortal ladder, then the beyond-immortality ladder ending in a deliberately undescribable final realm.
- **Realm variants**: breakthroughs are not linear. The same threshold can produce `Foundation Establishment`, `Heavenly Sword Foundation`, `Merchant's Golden Foundation`, `Broken Foundation`, `Endless Cycle Foundation`… depending on how the run was lived. The realm NAME becomes a record of the player's journey.
- Goal: two players can both say "I reached Golden Core" with completely different stories behind it — sword genius vs failed-merchant-turned-wandering-doctor — and both paths are valid. Cultivation is not "choose the strongest class"; it is "discover your Dao". This is where the roguelike side and the xianxia side reinforce each other.
- **This is the answer to the incremental-game staleness problem.** Most incrementals eventually become "you do the same thing, faster". The xianxia/roguelike framing escapes that because **every life can be a different novel** — not a re-run at higher speed but a genuinely different story. Illustrative build-lives: the Sword Genius (village → martial school → sword sect → Foundation → dies young), the Merchant Sage (trading → alchemy ingredients → formation merchant → rich immortal), the Healer (medicine → body refinement → life extension → immortality through understanding life), the Mad Experimenter (forbidden techniques → demonic cultivation → reality distortion → transcendence). All valid; none is "the optimal class".
- Late mortal realms retroactively reframe early ones: at Mortal Transcendent the player learns the "internal force" they cultivated was primitive qi all along (see the reinterpretation pillar in `identity.md`).

## Macro Phase 1 — Mortal World (Wuxia)

The player believes this is the whole universe.

1. **Ordinary Person** — the true beginning. No martial arts. Backgrounds (farmer, merchant, scholar, soldier, craftsman); the player doesn't know there are "builds".
2. **Body Tempering** — the first cultivation-like stage, still read as "this person trains extremely hard". Muscles, bones, endurance, reflexes.
3. **Blood Refinement** — the body becomes extraordinary: survive injuries, stamina, vitality. Legends begin.
4. **Meridian Opening** — the key threshold: the player discovers *internal energy exists*. Not yet called qi — breathing techniques, internal force. Very wuxia.
5. **Internal Force** — the classic wuxia master: palm strikes, enhanced senses, light-body techniques. The world considers them monsters.
6. **True Martial** — the peak of wuxia: sword intent, killing aura, weapon mastery. One person can defeat armies.
7. **Martial Grandmaster** — the apparent ceiling. The player thinks "I have reached the end". They have not.
8. **Martial Saint** — one per generation; walking on water, cutting mountains. Worshipped.
9. **Mortal Transcendent** — the final mortal boundary and the reveal: internal force *was* primitive qi.

## Macro Phase 2 — Cultivation World

The veil breaks: the world is filled with spiritual energy.

- **Qi Condensation** (Early / Middle / Late / Peak) — from barely-above-martial-master to rivaling sect elders.
- **Foundation Establishment** — a massive breakthrough; the cultivator *creates their path*. One of the largest events in the game. Foundations: Sword, Body, Pill, Formation, Beast, Merchant/Fortune, Karma… (prime variant territory).
- **Core Formation** (False Core / Golden Core / Perfect Core) — where luck/fate matters: high heavenly luck can yield flawless cores and hidden opportunities.
- **Nascent Soul** — the soul becomes independent; death changes meaning ("life is not the end").
- **Spirit Transformation** — spiritual clones, remote perception, domains. Connects to the meta layer: the cultivator becomes less tied to one body — which the player already does through reincarnation.
- **Void Refinement** — space manipulation, dimensional storage, crossing worlds.
- **Body Integration** — body, soul, and Dao merge.
- **Tribulation Transcendence** — the heavens test you. The major prestige mechanic of this phase.

## Macro Phase 3 — Immortal World

Immortality is NOT the end — many xianxia stories actually begin here.

Human Immortal → Earth Immortal → Heaven Immortal → Golden Immortal → Taiyi Golden Immortal → **Da Luo Golden Immortal** (past, present and future connect — the prestige layers get very interesting here: the player can affect previous lives).

## Macro Phase 4 — Beyond Immortality

Leaving traditional cultivation:

Dao Comprehension (Sword Dao, Time Dao, Fate Dao…) → Dao Integration (you no longer practice a law, you ARE one) → Dao Lord (you create laws) → Heavenly Dao (you understand the system governing reality) → Beyond Heavenly Dao (the realization: the Heavenly Dao itself was a realm) → Origin Realm → Transcendent Being → **??? Realm** — the final unknown, where mechanics, narrative, UI, and reality become the same thing. This is where the game can become truly meta (see `meta-design.md`).
